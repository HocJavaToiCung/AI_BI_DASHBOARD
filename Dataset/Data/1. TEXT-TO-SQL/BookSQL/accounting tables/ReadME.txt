1. master_txn_table.csv - It is the main table which consists of all the transactions record that happened in the businesses.
2. chart_of_accounts.csv - It consists of all the accounts names and their types of all the businesses.
3. products_service.csv - It consists of all the products/services and their types used by businesses.
4. customers.csv - It consists the record of all the customers and their details like name, billing and shipping address, etc which are associated with the businesses.
5. vendors.csv - It consists the record of all the vendors and their details like name, billing and shipping address, etc which are associated with the businesses.
6. payment_method.csv - It consists of the payment methods used by the businesses.
7. employees.csv - It consists of the details of employees such as name, employee ID, hire date, etc working in particular business.
